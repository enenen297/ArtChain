How ArtChain works

A simple three-step workflow for establishing digital artwork provenance.

01

Generate a digital fingerprint

Upload your artwork and the system generates a unique 64-character SHA-256 hash based on the exact contents of the file.

02

Store the artwork

The artwork and its associated metadata are stored through IPFS via Pinata, while the artwork's cryptographic fingerprint is recorded on-chain.

03

Create a provenance record

A smart contract records the artwork's hash, creator wallet address, and registration timestamp, creating an immutable record that can be independently verified.
